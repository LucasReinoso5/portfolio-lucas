ProMind

A role-based system that turns organizational knowledge needs into prioritized, validated, and reusable knowledge.

Year

Focus
Process design & AI-assisted prototyping

01 — The challenge

From isolated requests to reusable knowledge

ProWhy supports structured problem-solving through approaches such as 8D and PDCA. ProMind was conceived as a complementary system to answer the next question: how can the knowledge required or produced during those processes be captured and reused?

The challenge was to organize the full lifecycle of a knowledge request without creating knowledge automatically or bypassing business and technical validation.

02 — My role

Process owner and prototype creator

I independently analyzed the problem, defined the actors and business rules, modeled the workflow, translated it into functional requirements, and built the resulting web prototype.

AI-assisted development

The application was created with Codex. I defined the required behavior, workflows, roles, and corrections, then iteratively tested and refined the generated implementation until it represented the designed process.

03 — Approach

Model the process before the interface

  • Defined four operational roles: Detector, Knowledge Manager, Knowledge Creator, and Technical Reviewer.
  • Mapped the states and decision points from the initial need to final knowledge storage.
  • Converted the process into modules for requests, backlog management, K-Sprints, validation, and the knowledge base.
  • Designed prioritization around criticality, estimated workload, and available K-Sprint capacity.
  • Included rejection, correction, and reprioritization cycles rather than treating the workflow as strictly linear.

04 — Implementation

A working role-based application

The prototype supports authentication, role-specific workspaces, request tracking, business validation, prioritization, capacity-constrained K-Sprint generation, knowledge creation, technical review, and final storage in a reusable knowledge base.

05 — Outcome

Business logic made tangible

The project produced a coherent process model and a first functional application that demonstrates it end to end. The prototype makes responsibilities visible, connects prioritization to available resources, and provides traceability from an initial request to validated organizational knowledge.

06 — What I learned

Software begins with decisions

ProMind showed me how process analysis, requirements definition, and iterative validation can turn domain knowledge into an operational system. It also gave me practical experience using AI as a development tool while retaining responsibility for the product logic and final behavior.
